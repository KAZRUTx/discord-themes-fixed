How to install

Step-by-step guide for Vencord & BetterDiscord →

Quick Vencord solution: click Copy Vencord link, then go to Settings → Vencord → Themes → Online Themes and paste it. The theme updates itself.
BetterDiscord: click Download and put the file in %appdata%\BetterDiscord\themes.

Stuck, or a theme looks broken? Ask in #theme-support on Discord or open an issue.
